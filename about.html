Meet Nina Thompson

Enchanted Locks was created by Nina Thompson, a passionate hairdresser who loves bold styles, creativity and positive energy.

Nina wanted to create a salon where people could relax, experiment with their style and leave feeling confident.

Our Story

Enchanted Locks started with a simple idea: hair appointments should be enjoyable, welcoming and personal.

The salon combines professional hairdressing with a fun and friendly atmosphere.
